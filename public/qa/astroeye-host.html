    What this means

    “Detected” means a prerequisite exists, not that the feature works end to end. Storage persistence, microphone permissions, voice recognition, provider access, chart accuracy, embedded-host behavior and performance still need separate tests.

    Missing voice features do not by themselves prevent manual AstroEye use. If graphics, storage APIs or named time zones are missing, manual use may also be affected.

    The report contains only these checks, not your browser identity, location, keys or records. Nothing is sent automatically.
